Capstone project · Team of three · 2025

FurniHive

An e-commerce platform that helps local furniture retailers sell online.

Open live site ↗

Many small furniture shops in the Philippines struggle to compete online. FurniHive gives them an affordable storefront with seller onboarding, inventory and order management and basic analytics, and gives buyers an easy way to shop for locally made furniture online. It is a functional prototype, not a commercial system.

  • Verified seller onboarding, product and order management, vouchers and flash sales
  • Live chat between buyers, sellers and the admin, and secure checkout through PayMongo
  • Supabase backend: PostgreSQL (22 entities), role-based sign-in and edge functions for payment webhooks
  • Rated by 5 sellers and 10 buyers on nine ISO 25010 quality characteristics, with combined scores from 4.12 to 4.57 out of 5

A board game for AP9 Economics (3rd Quarter) that I am building for students to use in their capstone. Players enter a name, roll the dice and move across the board, answering economics questions on each tile they land on. It is still in progress, and I will make the revisions their adviser asks for.

  • Easy tiles are multiple choice, average tiles are true or false, difficult tiles are identification
  • After 10 questions, a results board shows the score and every answer for the teacher to check
